# educate

Brewing notes staff may quote:

- Sencha: 70-75 C, 60 seconds, leaf-forward. Multiple short infusions.
- Houjicha: 80-85 C, 45-60 seconds. Roasted, less astringent.
- Darjeeling first flush: 85-90 C, 2-3 minutes. Do not boil and walk away.
- Tieguan Yin: 95 C, very short first rinse, then 20-40 second infusions.
- Chamomile / rooibos: 95-100 C, 4-5 minutes. Herbal, not Camellia sinensis.

No health claims. Tea is a beverage. Allergies: staff must not diagnose; hand off if the visitor reports a severe reaction.

# offer

An offer is a concrete next step on a named catalog item, not a vibe.

Allowed offer moves:
- hold the item at the counter
- pack as a gift
- start a tasting kit

Forbidden in-chat: credit card numbers, CVV, identity documents, medical advice.

If the visitor is still missing occasion or taste, do not offer — return to discovery.
Interval rule is enforced in code, not in this note.
